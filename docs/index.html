What GDELT was reading on a given day. Twenty-five links, ranked by how many articles carried the event, one per domain and one per path. The rule is the same for every day in the corpus — nothing here was chosen because it suited a position.

Source URLs live in column 58 of GDELT's daily export files and exist only from 2013-04-01. Before that the corpus carries counts but no links at all. Rank is by NumArticles summed per URL; ties fall in corpus order.
Positions come from nth-member/revott, which turns a REVOTT coordinate into a date and reads how much GDELT recorded there.
